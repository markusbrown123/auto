# Hollow Reign: Higgsfield Prompt Library

**NOTE: Higgsfield was NOT available in this environment (no connector, API host unreachable). Nothing was generated. These are reusable prompts for later.**

Generated video and images are for **marketing, cinematics and concept art only**. They never substitute for interactive combat, and App Store screenshots/previews must show real gameplay captures (Guideline 2.3). Do not claim generated material is gameplay.

## Shared style block (prepend to every prompt)
> STYLE: realistic medieval dark fantasy, grounded and physical, desaturated palette of blackened steel, faded gold, deep red and grey stone. Volumetric fog, drifting embers, falling rain, wet stone, soft low-key lighting, cinematic film grain, shallow depth of field. No logos, no text, no watermarks, no UI. No resemblance to any existing game, film or franchise characters. Original designs only.

Negative: text, captions, logos, watermark, modern objects, anime, cartoon, neon, bright saturation, extra limbs, deformed hands, existing game characters.

## Character reference sheets
Generate each as: "character turnaround sheet, front, side, back, three-quarter, neutral A-pose, plain grey background" + the block below, then reuse the image as a reference for all later shots to keep the design consistent.

**The Returned Knight (player):** battered plate armour with dents and scratches, tarnished faded-gold trim, tattered dark cape, closed helm with a narrow visor slit, worn leather straps, a plain heavy greatsword with a dark wrapped grip, human proportions, lean and weary stance.

**The Ashen Sentinel:** towering knight, 2.5 m tall, blackened plate coated with ash, faint ember glow in joints and visor, massive two-handed sword with a cracked blade, slow heavy bearing.

**The Hollow Executioner:** broad hooded figure, rough dark linen hood hiding the face, scarred leather and iron apron, heavy single-edged cleaver as tall as a man, chains at the belt.

**The Crimson Widow:** slender armoured duelist, articulated fitted plate, twin curved blades, crimson veil over a narrow helm, tattered red sash, poised and fluid stance.

**The Cathedral Beast:** hulking clawed guardian in broken ceremonial armour, cracked stone-grey hide, shattered gilded breastplate, antler-like crest fragments, hunched on knuckles.

**The Frostbound King:** armour frozen with rime and ice crystals, frost greatsword with a pale-blue edge, crown of jagged ice, breath fog, still regal posture.

**The Forgotten Monarch (final):** gaunt ruler in tarnished gold armour, hollow crown with an empty centre, tattered royal mantle in faded crimson, a thin ceremonial blade, hollow dark eyes, severe stillness.

## Regions (establishing shots, 16:9, wide, no characters unless noted)
1. **The Fallen Courtyard:** collapsed castle courtyard, broken statues, scattered shields, wet cobbles, light rain, a distant gate, grey dawn.
2. **The Blackwood:** dead black forest, twisted trunks, ground fog, torn banners on branches, faint ember lights between trees.
3. **The Cathedral of Ash:** ruined cathedral nave, shattered rose window, ash drifting like snow, burnt pews, shafts of dim light.
4. **The Sunken Crypt:** flooded stone crypt, knee-deep black water, carved sarcophagi, guttering torches, green-grey damp light.
5. **The Frozen Citadel:** ice-locked fortress on a cliff, wind-blown snow, frozen banners, hard blue-grey light, frost on armour statues.
6. **The Hollow Throne:** vast empty throne hall, towering columns, tarnished gold throne under a cracked dome, dust and embers in a single beam.

Prompt template: `[STYLE] Establishing shot of [region description], cinematic wide angle, slow camera push-in, 5 seconds.`
