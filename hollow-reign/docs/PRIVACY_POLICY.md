## Summary
Hollow Reign does not collect, store on a server, share, or sell any personal data.

## What we do not do
- No accounts or sign-in
- No analytics or crash-reporting services
- No advertising and no ad or tracking SDKs
- No in-app purchases
- No network connection is required to play

## What stays on your device
Game progress, settings, and saved data are stored locally on your device. We cannot see them. Deleting the app removes them. If you use iCloud or device backups, your device settings govern whether the save file is included, and we do not access it.

## Children
The game contains fantasy violence and carries an age rating chosen in the App Store (expected 13+). We do not knowingly collect data from anyone, including children.
