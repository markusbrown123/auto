# World history

| Era | What happened |
|---|---|
| The Age of Ascension | Humanity learns to move a mind between artificial bodies. The machines that do it, together called the **Eternal Crown**, are built to preserve life and end up granting the rich and powerful functional immortality. They become the **Everbound**. |
| The Age of Crowns | The Everbound found kingdoms and rule for centuries. Their technology drifts out of ordinary reach until they are worshipped as gods. Their castles are both palaces and guarded facilities; their guardians, engineered beasts and armies enforce their rule. The dragons are bred in this era as living weapons. |
| The War of Broken Thrones | The mortal kingdoms rebel. After generations the immortal realms fall and the Crown network is fractured. Some Everbound are cut off for good; others hide or are trapped inside their own resurrection systems. |
| The Forgotten Centuries | More than three hundred years. People rebuild in the ruins and forget what the ruins were. Armour becomes legend, weapons become relics, machinery becomes myth and religion. |
| The Great Silence | Nineteen years before the game. The Crown wakes without warning. Settlements lose contact, villages vanish, guardians stir, and machines begin rebuilding warriors dead for centuries. A signal starts broadcasting from the old capital. People call it the **Hollow Reign**. |
| Now | The Unbound wakes. |

The kingdom explored in the game is **Vaelora**. It was one of many; the network reached far beyond it. That is the hook for later stories and is not explained in this game.

## What things really are
| What people see | What it was |
|---|---|
| Castles and keeps | Residences built over laboratories and network facilities |
| Temples and cathedrals | Consciousness-transfer and processing halls |
| Holy monuments and spires | Communication towers |
| Relic swords | Weapons with their own power systems |
| Dragons | Engineered war creatures that outlived their makers |
| Sanctuary candle, shrines | Still-working maintenance and repair points |
| Resurrection | A chamber rebuilding the Unbound's body from its record |
