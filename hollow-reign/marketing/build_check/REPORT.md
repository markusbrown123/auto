# Build check before the iPhone build (2026-11-27)

Verification only. No file under `src/`, `data/`, `assets/`, `tests/`, `project.godot` or `.import` was touched by this check, and no git
command was run. Items 1 and 3 were re-recorded after the coordinator's fixes (commit ab4e984). Both the before and after clips are kept.

Method: real-time recordings at a fixed 30 fps (Godot `--fixed-fps 30 --write-movie`, xvfb + lavapipe, as in `tools/record.sh`)
of the unchanged game. A small observer run from outside with `godot -s` (`tools/bc_wrap.gd.txt` + `bc_probe.gd.txt`) logs the sim
(HP, stamina, shield charges, every event), the models on screen and the walker's position. For item 4 only, it also presses the road's own
buttons. Drivers reused: `src/dev/combat_demo.gd` (`--combat`, `--flurry_every`), `showcase.gd` (`--showcase=block`),
`trail_demo.gd` (`--screen=trails`), `prompt_demo.gd` (`--screen=prompts`). Clips are 640 px wide, crf 27, all under 3.2 MB. Sheets are cut
from the 960x540 originals. Event logs are in `logs/`. Software renderer only: nothing here is a claim about iPhone performance.

## Open findings

1. **Blue starting shield** (item 6): `assets/models/s_plank_Color_*.jpg` (Splintered Heater) is blue and shows navy in
   character select and on the road. Nothing in code greys it.
2. **Guard charges** (item 2, design caveat): with three charges and no refill while guarding, the 4th held block in a
   row breaks and costs HP at nearly full stamina.
3. Fixed and re-verified: the queue of rapid swipes into an open foe (item 1) and the counter seam drawn as the large line (item 3).
